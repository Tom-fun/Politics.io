PARLIAMENT AT A GLANCE

Two chambers. Different jobs.

Australia’s federal Parliament has a House of Representatives and a Senate. Members and senators represent electorates, states or territories.

H

House of Representatives

150 seats

The House is where government is formed. Members represent individual federal electorates and debate proposed laws, budgets and public policy.

Official House information ↗
S

Senate

76 seats

Senators represent states and territories. The Senate reviews legislation and often has a broader mix of parties and independents.

THE BASICS

How a federal decision can become law

01

Idea or bill

A bill is introduced into Parliament and its purpose is explained.

02

Debate

Members or senators debate the bill and may propose amendments.

03

Both chambers

In most cases, both the House and Senate must agree on the final text.

04

Royal assent

The Governor-General gives Royal Assent before an Act becomes law, subject to its commencement arrangements.
